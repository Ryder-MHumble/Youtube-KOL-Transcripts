23|## 一、开场就把"tier one names"当筹码——一支成长基金的政治姿态
24|
25|访谈开场不到一分钟就抛出基金和组合名单，节奏刻意得近乎挑衅：
26|
27|> "we're officially announcing the hundred million oversubscribed growth fund and some of the tier one names, maybe all the tier one names… Anduril, Etched, Cognition, SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic, Saronic, Modal." [≈00:30]
28|
29|这里值得拆的不是名单，而是修饰语。**"some of the tier one names, maybe all the tier one names"** 这句带笑意的含糊，是一次刻意的降维宣示——它把一支规模在 a16z 体系里只算中等偏小的基金，挂到了与顶级机构"同台"的位置上。成长基金（growth fund）而非早期 VC 基金这个定位本身也透露信息：Anti Fund 不打算和 a16z 自家的早期基金正面竞争，而是占据"已经跑出来、估值仍在上行"的少数头部公司——OpenAI、Anthropic、Anduril、SpaceX——这一窄缝。**这是一笔关于"幂律尾部已经确定、剩下只是分配权争夺"的押注。**
30|
31|Geoff Woo 紧接着被要求描述 Jake 的"超能力"，他给出了本场最关键的定性之一：
32|
33|> "I think Jake from the outside could be like entertainer or professional athlete, but I think to me Jake's always been a constant entrepreneur… he's really created himself as a taste maker and a cultural leader of his generation… all the young kids today look up and want to be an influencer. Jake literally created the influencer term in that like job profile." [≈02:00]
34|
35|把"创造了 influencer 这个职业"作为核心资产来陈述，等于宣告：Anti Fund 的差异化不来自研究能力，而来自 Jake 对一代人文化偏好的塑造力。a16z 主持人紧接着套用 a16z 自己"launch a bunch of stuff and pretend it never happened"的玩笑来承接 Jake 的"失败时扫地毯下"叙事，**这本身就是 a16z 在给 Jake 发一张入伙通行证**——把一个曾被硅谷视为异类的人接纳为同类。

39|## 二、"两个杠杆"——把注意力抬升到与资本并列的资产类别
40|
41|全场最具有思想密度的一段，是 Geoff Woo 对"为什么 Jake 是理想合伙人"的结构性论证。他先给出一套朴素但锋利的资本哲学：
42|
43|> "managing $1 to $100 to a million dollars to a billion dollars is kind of the same level of hard… the best investors are just really really strong conviction folks who just are making big bets." [≈20:00]
44|
45|接着他把这套哲学推向本场真正的论点——**资本与注意力是文明的两条平行杠杆**：
46|
47|> "science, computer science, physics are foundational to just the nature of reality, and then capital is the lever of human society and civilization… where anti-fund has been quite unique is that in a world where there's more and more capital, attention [is] more scarce, mind share more scarce… you really can't name a better partner than Jake Paul in terms of wielding attention and driving cultural taste. So if we can really master those two levers maybe we can control the world." [≈22:00]
48|
49|这段话的隐含立场非常值得警惕。它把"资本"和"注意力"并列为社会两大杠杆，但**刻意省略了第三条传统杠杆——制度/政策权力**（直到后半场 Jake 谈政治时才补上）。这是硅谷当下流行的"techno-capitalist"叙事的简化版：押注资本与mind share的复利，绕开民主问责。Geoff 末尾那句"let's just try and have someone stop us"是这种叙事最直白的姿态性表达。
50|
51|a16z 主持人随即把它接到一个广为流传的梗——"if you're smart, pivot to being hot"，并逼出 Geoff 对"AI maxing vs looks maxing"的 AGI 时代阐释：
52|
53|> "if we assume that we're pretty AGI pilled, intelligence is just going to be metered out through compute, then what matters then it's like your relationship with people… looks maxing is a pretty narrow focus… but I think it's beyond just looks. It's your vibe, your ability to just be charismatic and engage with people. So that's like an important skill set in the future. Can you just have better vibes than everyone else?" [≈24:30]
54|
55|这是本场最该被产品经理抄走的一句判断。**它把 AGI 商品化智能的后果翻译成了一个产品策略问题：当"智能"由 compute 供给、近乎免费时，差异化迁移到"vibe/关系/魅力"这条非可计算轴上。** Geoff 甚至当众奉承 a16z："what you've done with the media strategy for Andreessen Horowitz, you guys are literally executing on looks maxing the VC world… you have your own propaganda arm just pushing out your view of the world." [≈25:30]——把 a16z 的媒体机器命名为"looks maxing 的 VC 版本"，是这场访谈里硅谷内部最不留情面的一次自指。
56|
57|对 AI 产品经理的直接行动含义：（a）产品差异化重心应从"能力堆叠"迁移到"人格/风格/社区引力"——后者更难被基础模型复制；（b）"vibe"作为产品维度需要被显性度量和投入，而不是作为美学副产品被忽略；（c）当主持人问"22 岁在 2026 该做什么"时，Geoff 给出的反潮流答案是"deep into math, computer science"，理由是：
58|
59|> "if you actually know underlying how these large language models are actually working, you actually can wield them and prompt them better… the people that are leading these industries are like math champions." [≈28:00]
60|
61|这是一条看似与"vibe 时代"矛盾、实则互补的双轴建议：**要么 max IQ（深度掌握模型机制，成为少数能驾驭底层的人），要么 max EQ/vibe（成为稀缺真实人格）——中间地带被 AGI 抹平。**

65|## 三、"媒体打法已关闭"——幂律尾部与"真实人格的稀缺溢价"
66|
67|主持人问 Jake：你那套 YouTube 打法今天还能复制吗？Jake 给出了一个外部观察者很少能从头部创作者口中听到的明确否定：
68|
69|> "No, I don't think it would… there's very few people breaking through on the media side of things. It's a lot harder to grow, a lot harder to amass a following… you kind of had to be there from the start. And that's why you really see with brands that have stuck around at the highest level in social media, it's me, Mr. Beast, and my brother, and there's really no one else." [≈32:30]
70|
71|**"it's me, Mr. Beast, and my brother, and there's really no one else"**——这是一句带有强既得利益色彩的判断，但它指向一个真实结构：内容分发已从"增量平台红利"转入"存量幂律固化"。Geoff 把它接到投资框架上：
72|
73|> "I think to me Jake is a startup in and of itself, and in startup land it's all power law… Jake has hit escape velocity. So I think Jake gets even more powerful in this new era of AI generated characters and just infinite noise… you'll have a lot of AI generated content and the people that you grew up watching, loving and just supporting or hating as a child… that generation is now going to take over from the boomers who are going to be dying off… I think you compound just like in OpenAI and Anthropic are compounding even faster." [≈38:00]
74|
75|> "there's going to be fewer real people that you care about." [≈38:30]
76|
77|这段是本场最具投资含义的判断之一：**AI 生成内容泛滥 → 真实人格稀缺 → 已建立"escape velocity"的真实人格获得稀缺溢价**。它直接对应 Anti Fund 选择 Jake 作为 GP 的底层逻辑——Jake 本身就是一支"已 escape velocity 的人格股票"。Geoff 紧接着把 Jake 与 MrBeast 做了一个尖锐的差异化对比，这是本场最锋利的产品判断：
78|
79|> "Mr. Beast is so quantitative and analytical that it doesn't matter if Jimmy's in a video or not. Like swap him out for another game show host… I don't think Jimmy would say that he's even that smooth of a natural actor or character… where I think Jake has a lot of durability is that you follow his story… if Jake can just talk and get a bunch of views or just live his life and get a bunch of views and someone has to light $100 million on fire to get a bunch of views, who do I want to be partners with?" [≈34:30]
80|
81|**"light $100 million on fire to get a bunch of views" vs "just live his life and get views"** 这组对比，把 MrBeast 的工作流（高资本、高分析、可替换主持人）与 Jake 的工作流（低资本、人格驱动、不可替换）映射成两种资产类型。Geoff 的隐含判断是：**前者是内容工厂，后者是人格特许经营权**；在 AI 内容冲击下，后者的护城河更宽。这是对创作者经济估值方法论的一次暗中重写——按"是否可被替换主持人"来给创作者定价。
82|
83|Jake 自己补上货币化这一刀：
84|
85|> "I think a lot of people in the space can have a lot of followers, but not the cash, and cash is king… a lot of people in the space don't know how to actually pull cash out." [≈37:00]
86|
87|> "things now have to be like 10, 20, 30 million dollar opportunities for me to spend time on it." [≈40:30]
88|
89|第二句是一个头部创作者对自身机会成本的公开定价——**$10–30M 机会门槛**意味着他已把娱乐业务的边际机会系统性让位给资本配置业务。这是 Anti Fund 诞生的内部财务前提。

93|## 四、反脆弱被显性化为筛选指标——但隐含了一处立场回避
94|
95|主持人顺势把"resilience / pain tolerance"作为 Jake 的可教学技能来提问。Jake 给出的起源叙事含两块：（1）"非常严厉的父亲"；（2）从高中起全校匿名账号、校长老师一起攻击他的"被恨"环境。他的结论是"it's always been there, so I'm just used to it"——把反脆弱归因为早期暴露脱敏。Geoff 把它抬到筛选框架高度：
96|
97|> "if you're literally fighting and punching people or getting punched in the face for a living, and then the internet punching you… if you can survive that and thrive in that environment, that's presidential like head of state level courage and resiliency… that human quality that is very rare is inspiring to me and hopefully for the startup founders and our portfolio companies." [≈14:00]
98|
99|**这是一次把"被互联网群殴过并活下来"显性化为创始人尽调指标的尝试。** 它的真实含义是：在 AI 时代舆论冲击波放大、创始人被"cancelled"概率上升的背景下，"抗网暴能力"成为一项新的 diligence 维度。
100|
101|Jake 紧接着抛出一个全场传播力最强的金句，被反复引用：
102|
103|> "Everything you do has risk. Just don't take fatal risk… don't get yourself killed and probably don't go to jail… don't die, and as long as you take risks and calculated bets and keep compounding you'll eventually end up somewhere good… most people are just too afraid to do anything that even warrants press… most people can't even be cancelled because you didn't exist." [≈42:30]
104|
105|最后一句是最锋利的：**"most people can't even be cancelled because you didn't exist"**——把"被取消焦虑"重新框定为一种"尚未取得存在感"的奢侈品焦虑。这是一个反共识的、对 cancel culture 论述的降维打击，也是 Jake 整套反脆弱哲学最政治不正确、却最难反驳的一环。
106|
107|**回避之处值得点出**：当主持人问"all press is good press?"，Jake 回答"for certain people, for me yes, because I can survive it"——这是"抗 fragile"个体视角的答案；但他回避了集体外部性维度。他自己在下一段承认了这一点：
108|
109|> "the streaming culture is like really interesting and probably not the best for society… I feel bad for like sort of contributing to where this has gone but at the time I like never thought of that… I set the bar here as crazy and now the streamers have to do it every day for multiple hours… to keep people's attention they're having to do out of pocket shit, which I don't think is like a good influence." [≈45:00]
110|
111|> "I think it's probably already near its peak because the platforms now like aren't allowing certain things to be streamed and they're already getting like deplatformed." [≈46:00]
112|
113|**这里出现一个结构性立场张力**：Jake 一方面把"驾驭注意力的能力"作为 Anti Fund 的核心资产定价，另一方面又承认这种注意力经济的负面外部性、并预测监管（平台 deplatforming）会成为它的天花板。**这是一个把自身商业模式的外部性承认出来、却不准备内部化承担的典型硅谷立场**——和 a16z 长期对 crypto、对 AI 安全的态度同构。

117|## 五、信仰作为反脆弱的燃料——"right mission"作为筛选器的另一半
118|
119|当被问"如何从被 cancel 中反弹"，Jake 给出一条信仰/宿命论路径：
120|
121|> "faith and God, I think God gives its toughest battles to his most important soldiers… everything happens for a reason… you can't victimize yourself and know that if you come out on the other side of it, you will be stronger." [≈48:00]
122|
123|Geoff 把它接到一个更可投资的版本——把"信仰"重写为"对 mission 的自我信念"，并把它和 AI/国防使命绑定：
124|
125|> "if you think that you're banking beneficial AGI or you really believe like fusion energy or defense tech and making Americans have the best weapon systems as really important… having that strength to fight and battle for that makes it easier if you have that self-conviction that what you're doing is correct." [≈50:00]
126|
127|**这是一个值得警惕的修辞转化**：把宗教式信仰 → "对使命的确信" → "押注 AGI/国防这些具体赛道"用一句话缝起来。它在 Anti Fund 组合里得到了兑现（Anduril=国防、OpenAI/Anthropic=AGI）。其隐含判断是：**后真相时代的 founder defense 不再来自"被事实证明对"，而来自"被使命证明值得挨打"**——这对投资逻辑的实际含义是：在 AGI/国防这种长周期、不可证伪的赛道上，"founder 信念强度"比"商业指标"更值得作为持仓依据。这是一种非常适合用来锁定长期 illiquid 资产的叙事基础设施。

131|## 六、"biggest game in life"——从娱乐到政治的押注，与教育/金融素养的政策抓手
132|
133|后半场访谈的政治色彩陡升。Jake 不再以" entertainer"身份发言，而以"American"身份主张发声权：
134|
135|> "as an American, we all have our right to our opinion and to voice our opinion… with a lot of people following me, I've taken it upon myself to speak my mind on what I believe in… I think it's the biggest game in life." [≈53:00]
136|
137|主持人直接追问是否考虑参选，Jake 给出了一个有条件的"是"：
138|
139|> "I could see being involved for sure… President Trump like endorsed me on stage to run for president… I would only want to be involved in it if I was like the best person to do it… to help the country… if some other people on the other side of things that weren't good for the country felt like they had traction, I would probably do it to help the people of America and make sure we don't get wrapped up into this like whole socialist communist thing that's going on like in New York with [Mamdani]." [≈54:00]
140|
141|**"if the other side has traction, I would probably do it"**——这是一个"防御性参选"姿态：把自己包装成"反社会主义"的应急候选人。Geoff 用 Michael Jordan 的"Republicans buy shoes too"对照来赞美 Jake 的"愿意牺牲商业可选项性"。这一段把 Jake 从"创作者"叙事完全切到"政治野心家"叙事，是本场弧线的最大转折。
142|
143|Jake 紧接着给出他真正关心的政策抓手——**教育改革为第一优先**：
144|
145|> "education first and foremost… it's bad. it hasn't been reformed since the 1920s the 1930s… I think the number one problem out there right now with young men and young women even is like how do they make money?… actually learn about maybe how to do taxes in school and not about the Pythagorean theorem." [≈56:00]
146|
147|Geoff 接力给出一个具体的政策押注——**Trump 账户 / Invest America 账户**：
148|
149|> "the Invest America accounts, the Trump accounts… allowing the next generation of young Americans actually participate with the fastest growing private companies and having them be a shareholder and a stakeholder in success of American innovation is really important… having every little kid see their little stock portfolio and realizing, hey, this is how money and investing works." [≈58:00]
150|
151|**这条政策路径的真实含义是把"全民股票账户"嵌入新生儿身份——让下一代从出生起就持有美国创新公司的股权。** 它在 Anti Fund 的利益结构上是自洽的：让年轻人成为 OpenAI/Anduril 的早期股东，既是"金融素养教育"，也是把下一代流量导流到 Anti Fund 组合公司的供给侧基础设施。**教育政策与投资组合在此处合流——这是一条值得长期跟踪的"政策-资本-流量"三位一体押注。**
152|
153|访谈末段的"该不该上大学"辩论是这条线的延续。Geoff 给出本场最具实操性的判断之一——**"badges"理论**：
154|
155|> "Peter Thiel in some sense is like kind of hypocritical when he's like don't go to college where he had Stanford undergrad, Stanford Law School… getting a badge is useful if you don't have any other badges… Jake could not have to have the institutional badge of being educated because he became world class at what he does… but if you're not like Jake, then you need to get some external badges… collect some badges as a kid right—become the best YouTuber or go to Stanford, go to Harvard." [≈59:30]
156|
157|> "the educational content will be reformed, but the badging system will not change." [≈59:45]
158|
159|**最后一句是全场对"教育 vs 学历"之争最精准的一刀**：内容会被 AI 重写，但"外部背书系统"不会——因为它本质是信号博弈而非知识传递。这等于在说：**在 AGI 时代，学历的价值不在内容而在信号，且信号价值会相对上升**——因为当内容变得无限供给，可验证的外部稀缺信号反而溢价。这与第二章的"真实人格稀缺溢价"是同一套逻辑的另一面。

163|## 七、收尾暗线——founder taste 是"吨的 reps"，而 OpenAI 一周内复制你的风险
164|
165|访谈最后回到"founder taste 如何培养"。Geoff 的答案是去神秘化的：
166|
167|> "we just got a lot of reps at meeting people, getting pitched things, doing bad deals, making mistakes… a lot of it is probably just a ton of reps… Jake just had a lot of reps on a lot of stuff that 50-year-old men never deal with… he lives in dog years." [≈61:00]
168|
169|> "do they have a passion and a reason to believe why they can be world class? And then two, are they resilient and tough enough to eat a ton of shit to get there?… reference checks are also very important for us… one of the companies we invested in like a defense tech, we just like hit up Palmer Luckey's team and we're like, 'Yo, what do you think of this group?'" [≈61:30]
170|
171|**两条筛选轴 + 一条尽调方法**：轴一是"成为世界级的理由"，轴二是"吃shit耐受度"，尽调方法是"找赛道内部人（如 Palmer Luckey 团队）做 reference check"。这是一个可被早期投资人直接拷贝的 founder diligence 模板。
172|

189|## 弧：四层维度的扫描结论
190|
191|- **范式判断**：访谈认定的根本性变化是——智能被 AGI 商品化、资本因泛滥而贬值、注意力与真实人格相对稀缺。Jake Paul 押注"文化杠杆"会与"资本杠杆"并列，且在 AI 噪声时代溢价。
192|- **产业信号**：被淘汰——通用内容创作打法、应用层 AI 创业的"周级"护城河、可被替换主持人的内容工厂；在兴起——已 escape velocity 的真实人格特许权、成长基金对少数头部公司的分配权争夺、"教育内容重写 + 学历信号溢价"双轨、创作者→持牌金融的资产负债表化。
193|- **隐含立场**：说了——把注意力列为第二杠杆、把信仰转化为 mission 筛选器、政策与组合公司利益合流；没说——绕开了"资本+注意力"双杠杆绕开民主问责的伦理问题，绕开了注意力经济负面外部性的内部化责任，绕开了 Anti Fund 作为成长基金对二级市场流动性/估值透支的依赖。
194|- **可行动洞察（对 AI PM）**：（1）产品差异化重心从"能力"迁移到"vibe/人格/社区"这条非可计算轴；（2）应用层 AI 产品的护城河窗口以周计，须把"被基础模型复制"作为头号 risk 写进 PRD；（3）"被网暴并活下来"作为创始人/合作伙伴尽调维度的新合法性上升；（4）学历/证书作为"信号"的相对价值在 AI 时代上升而非下降——不要被"上大学无用论"叙事误导；（5）顶级创作者正在向持牌金融与资本配置迁移，PM 在做 BD/合作时按"是否已 escape velocity"和"是否可替换主持人"对创作者分层定价。
